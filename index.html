Project statement

An interactive casebook following Alex, a fictional Cornell student, through one ordinary day that becomes an investigation of social behavior. At each stop, readers make a prediction before uncovering the psychological explanation and a related finding from PSYCH 2800. The project connects egocentrism and the spotlight effect; conformity and pluralistic ignorance; construal and framing; situationism and attribution; questionable belief and overconfidence; channel factors; cognitive dissonance; emotion and misattribution; and social facilitation. Its central argument is that the same action can look very different once we examine the situation, the available evidence, and the perspective of the person making the judgment.

How to read this project

The characters and situations are fictional. The studies and psychological ideas come from PSYCH 2800. The choices invite readers to make predictions, not take a personality test.
